FishDex ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information about you when you use the FishDex mobile application.

01 Information We Collect

Account Information: Email address, username, display name, and profile photo when you create an account.

Catch Data: Photos, species, weight, length, location coordinates, and notes you log voluntarily. Before a photo leaves your phone we remove the location and other details stored inside the image file; the catch’s location is kept separately and only you, the host of a tournament you submit it to, and FishDex administrators can see it.

Fishing Preferences: What you tell us when you set up your account: your fishing styles, water types, experience level, how often you fish, favourite seasons, fishing goals, the species you target, and (if you choose to share it) your location during setup. We use these to suggest challenges, clubs and tournaments. Some of them (for example how often you fish) we do not use yet; we keep them with your profile and delete them when you delete your account.

Profile Details: The country and state or region you choose. Other users cannot see them; only you and FishDex administrators can.

Location Data: GPS coordinates you attach to catches or use when joining location-based clubs and tournaments. We never collect background location without your explicit permission.

Usage Data: We do not use analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites. Our service provider keeps ordinary server logs (for example the time of a request and the address it came from) to run and secure the service.

Communications: Messages sent within club feeds, league chats, and tournament chats.

Records of Your Agreements: Which version of these Terms and this Privacy Policy you accepted, and when, and when you accepted the community guidelines. When you join a club, league or tournament, we keep the rules you agreed to and when; its creator or host can see that record.

Read Status: When you last opened a club, league or tournament chat, so the app can show you what is new on any of your devices.

02 How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the FishDex service
  • To display your catches, profile, and competition standings
  • To suggest challenges, clubs and tournaments that match your fishing preferences and location
  • To send password reset emails and service notifications
  • To investigate reports of policy violations and enforce our Terms of Service
FishDex administrators may review user-generated content — including messages, posts, catches, and competition submissions — when investigating reported violations. This review is performed solely for moderation and safety purposes.

03 How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information.

Public Profile: Your username, display name, and public catches are visible to other users. Finished tournament results, and the clubs, leagues and tournaments you take part in, are visible to the other people in them.

Photos: Photos you upload (catch photos, profile photos, and club, league and tournament images) are stored at a web address that is hard to guess. The app only shows them to people allowed to see them, but anyone who is given a photo’s address can open it.

Service Providers: We use Supabase for database, file storage and authentication. Your data is stored in the United States. They process data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations.

Legal: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

04 Data Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. If you delete your account (Settings > Delete Account), we delete your personal data straight away, and in any case within 30 days. That includes your catches and their photos, your profile photo and details, your comments, messages and posts, your club, league and tournament memberships, your preferences and challenge progress, and your join records.

A few things are handled differently, because other people depend on them:

  • Clubs and leagues you own pass to their longest-standing member. If nobody else is a member, the club or league is deleted.
  • Tournaments you host. Finished tournaments stay, because their results belong to everyone who competed; your name is replaced with “Deleted user”. A tournament with no other registered anglers is deleted. A tournament that is still running, or that has registered anglers, must first be handed to another participant or ended: you are shown this before you confirm, and can do it in the app.
  • Your results in finished tournaments stay so that other anglers’ standings do not change. Your name becomes “Deleted user”, and your photos, notes and catch locations are deleted. Your results in tournaments that are still running or have not started are removed.
  • Reports you made, or that were made about you, and what we decided, are kept (without your profile details) so we can protect other users.
  • Backups. Our database provider may keep encrypted backups for a limited time, at most 30 days, after which they are deleted. Deleted data can remain in a backup until then. We do not use a backup to bring a deleted account back, and if one is ever restored we delete the accounts that had been deleted again.
  • We may keep information that the law requires us to keep.

05 Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and associated data (in the app: Settings > Delete Account, or by contacting us)
  • Get a copy of your data (email us and we will send it)

Exercise these rights by contacting us at support.fishdex@gmail.com or through the in-app account settings.

06 Children's Privacy

FishDex is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

07 Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit and at rest. No system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

08 Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of material changes through the app. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
