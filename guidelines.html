FishDex is a community for anglers. These guidelines apply everywhere in the app: clubs, leagues, tournaments, chats, comments, catches and your profile. They are part of the Terms of Service.

01 Keep It Fishing

FishDex is a community for anglers. Share catches, tips, and fishing experiences.

02 Respect Everyone

No harassment, threats, hate speech, or bullying. Treat every angler, host and member with respect, including in tournament and league disputes.

03 Post Real Catches

Only post photos of actual catches. Fake or fraudulent submissions, including faked weights, lengths or photos in a league or tournament, may result in disqualification, content removal or account suspension.

04 No Inappropriate Content

No nudity, graphic violence, or sexually explicit material of any kind. No illegal content.

05 Report Violations

If you see something that violates these guidelines, use the Report button. We review all reports within 24 hours. You can also block users from their profile.

06 Privacy Matters

Do not share other people's personal information (phone number, address, exact location, private messages) without their consent. Do not share invite codes outside the people they are meant for.

07 Host Rules

Hosts of clubs, leagues and tournaments may add their own rules, which you agree to when you join. Host rules apply in addition to these guidelines and the Terms of Service, never instead of them.

08 Consequences

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Violations may result in content removal, removal from a club, league or tournament, disqualification, or account suspension.
